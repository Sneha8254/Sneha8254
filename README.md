## 🏆 Featured Projects

### **1. Banking Customer Churn Analysis** 🏦
- **Dataset:** 10,000 customers
- **Key Finding:** €1-2M retention opportunity
- **Skills:** Data analysis, customer segmentation, business impact calculation
- **GitHub:** [Day 8 Analysis](https://github.com/Sneha8254/Python-DS-Learning-Journey/blob/main/Day%208_%20Banking%20_Customer_%20Churn%20_Analysis%20.ipynb)

**Insight:** Customers age 45+ are 7 years older on average and churn more frequently. Richer customers paradoxically churn more (€91K avg vs €72K retained).

---

### **2. Credit Card Fraud Detection Analysis** 💳
- **Dataset:** 10,000 transactions
- **Key Finding:** €5,198.76 annual fraud impact
- **Skills:** Fraud pattern identification, class imbalance handling, detection strategy
- **GitHub:** [Day 9 Analysis](https://github.com/Sneha8254/Python-DS-Learning-Journey/blob/main/Day%209_%20Credit%20Card%20Fraud%20Detection.ipynb)

**Insight:** Extreme class imbalance (0.37% fraud) requires precision-recall metrics, not accuracy. Fraudsters steal slightly larger amounts (€104 vs €97 avg).

---

### **3. SQL JOINs: Fraud-Customer Linking** 🔗
- **Dataset:** 5 customers × 6 transactions × 3 fraud alerts
- **Key Finding:** Complete fraud management system enabled
- **Skills:** INNER, LEFT, RIGHT, FULL OUTER JOINs, multi-table analysis
- **GitHub:** [Day 10-11 Analysis](https://github.com/Sneha8254/Python-DS-Learning-Journey/tree/main/Day_10_SQL_JOINs)

**Insight:** Linking fraud to customer details reveals actionable profiles:
- Charlie (50, Bangalore): €200 fraud + HIGH alert
- Eve (35, Mumbai): CRITICAL alert (no fraud yet)
- Unknown Customer 6: Alert but not in system (security issue!)
